DDT2 OPERATIONS — COMPANION 0.6.11

1. Open http://127.0.0.1:27840/. In Teams & connection, check the published DDT2
   source, review it, then import. The bundled fallback has 12 teams and 60
   players but no invented bracket. Recheck the source after bracket updates.
   Import is deliberate; opening the desk never replaces tournament data.
2. Save exact in-game trainer aliases for registered players. Unknown aliases
   can also be mapped gate by gate during race review.
3. On Race Desk, choose a bracket match. Open Match Lineup and choose one Uma
   and outfit per trainer. Save the lineup once for all six races and the tiebreaker.
   To mark a player disqualified, check DQ beside their name and save the lineup. Clear DQ and save to reinstate them. This applies to the matchup broadcast only.
4. Under On Air, select the match and race. Click Broadcast This Race to switch the overlays.
5. Choose your own horseACT Saved races folder and enable watching. Existing
   files are ignored at watcher startup; import an older file manually.
   Capture next race attaches the next new valid export to the selected heat.
   Loading another heat disarms the old selection.
6. Let the race finish ON THE BROADCAST before approving. horseACT may save a
   full simulated result before playback ends. Review all 10 entrants, their
   registered players, the course and podium. Race points are 4 / 2 / 1.
   Enter an operator name and approve the heat. This changes local totals only.
7. Reaching 25 blocks further race approvals but does not declare a winner.
   Confirm the series outcome separately after review. A score/race change
   invalidates an earlier outcome confirmation. No automatic bracket advance.
8. In Race Review, choose a finalized race and select Edit result to correct player assignments. Confirm the correction to update the existing race. Broadcast This Race changes the live race selection.
9. After the first ten-runner capture is assigned, choose any of the ten under Uma Spotlight and click Show on broadcast. These choices stay with this matchup.
10. When a team reaches 25, open Finalize, review the matchup score and confirm the winner. Download match ZIP includes approved-results.json and the original synced capture for each approved race under races/. Give the ZIP to the organizer, who uploads it to imports/ and runs Import approved match with its filename.

OBS SOURCES
Use http://127.0.0.1:27840/overlay for the live tracker. The /matchup, /scoreboard, /race-result and /uma-highlight sources use 1920 x 1080. Download the stinger from Broadcast scenes, add an OBS Stinger transition, and choose a time of 850 ms. Select that transition for scene changes.

LOCAL STATE
%LOCALAPPDATA%/DominatorTournament/tournament.json stores this PC's event.
Raw imports are kept under raw/ by fingerprint. Duplicate exports cannot
score twice. Stop the companion before copying or restoring its state.
Each PC is independent; the website is the published view, not a multi-user
editing server. Demo overlays and the native sample preview never score.

BETWEEN-RACE SCENES
Use matchup and scoreboard as separate 1920 x 1080 OBS scenes.
Matchup shows both teams and five player/Uma panels per side.
Scoreboard columns R1-R6 and TB show cumulative points after each approved
race, with the points earned in that race underneath. The total reflects any
approved static override without rewriting the per-race progression.
The last approved race supplies three podium cards and that race's 4/2/1.
Pending results never appear. Add ?demo=1 to either URL to preview samples.
Remove ?demo=1 for broadcast. Preview mode never edits tournament state.
The Helper bundles full-body art and portraits for offline use. Exact outfit names come from the English game catalog.

MATCH MANAGEMENT
DDT2 uses the published bracket. Select a match and choose Reset match to clear its results and winner. The same fixture, teams, drafted tracks, and lineups remain available. Captured races can be reviewed again. Resetting a match does not publish changes.

UPDATING AN OPEN DESK
If an earlier Helper is still running after 0.6.11 is installed, save any pending edits and open the Tournament Helper shortcut. Choose Yes to switch versions. The launcher checks the running desk again before switching. The game and saved tournament stay in place.
